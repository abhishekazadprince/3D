Help & button guideDouble-tap the graph for full screen (double-tap again to exit). Corner buttons: 123 numbers, xα symbols, ▦ grid, ╋ fade axes, ◌ floating labels, ✦ glow/thickness, Aa label size, ◫ plane fill. Right wall: F physics-symbol view of ⚗ (vector symbols + arrows; press again for algebra), ⚛ aesthetic physics scene, ⚗ real-physics scene + laws panel (kept separate from ⚛), ◑ hide math layer, ⏯ pause, ⟳ rotate, ⌂ reset, ◩ view, ⤓ PNG. Only the ⚙ button is always visible: tap it to show or hide all the other buttons (it works in full screen too; double-tap the graph is the only way to enter or leave full screen). Long-press a point to read its coordinates; pinch to zoom. Step-by-step reveals the solution one row at a time. In ⚛ mode, Mass / Current / Speed sliders reshape the scene. ⚙ opens all view tools.
